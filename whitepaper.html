Abstract

NXT Cloud is a Web3 ecosystem made of three products: NXTPad, NXT DEX, and NXT Finance. Together they are meant to give creators, traders, and communities one connected place to launch projects, trade assets, and put capital to work.

This paper explains the vision behind NXT Cloud, the role each product plays, the principles that guide how we build, and where each product stands today. It is a living document and will change as the products do.

Vision

Web3 is fragmented. Launching a project usually means stitching together separate tools for each network. Liquidity is spread thin, and users repeat the same steps in a different interface every time they cross a chain.

NXT Cloud aims to reduce that friction. We are building products that share one identity and one experience, and that are designed from the start to work across networks instead of being tied to one.

The goal in one line: make it simple to build, launch, trade, and grow across chains from a single ecosystem.

NXTPad

NXTPad is the first product in the NXT Cloud ecosystem: a multi-chain launchpad. It gives new tokens, projects, and communities a place to launch without being locked into a single network, so a project can pick the chain that fits its audience.

NXTPad is nearing completion and is where the ecosystem begins. You can try it now at NXTPad.

Supported networks, launch mechanics, and fees will be published here as they are finalized.

NXT DEX

NXT DEX is a decentralized exchange being built for speed, security, and low fees. It is in active development, and cross-chain capabilities are a key part of the plan.

The exchange is intended to be the natural place to trade assets that launch through NXTPad, keeping the path from launch to trading inside one ecosystem.

NXT Finance

NXT Finance is our long-term vision: a suite of DeFi tools that can include lending, staking, and yield farming. It builds on the launchpad and the exchange, and it will be scoped in detail once those products are established.

Because it is furthest out, its scope may change. We will update this section as plans become concrete.

Principles

  • Security first. Security is treated as a requirement for launching a product, not something added afterward.
  • Honest status. Every product is labeled by its actual stage. We do not present something as live before it is.
  • Built for many chains. Products are designed to work across networks so users are not tied to one.
  • Community at the center. Creators and communities are the reason the ecosystem exists, and their needs shape what we build next.

Roadmap

We build in order and share progress by stage rather than by promised dates.

Now: NXTPad

Finish and release the multi-chain launchpad.

Next: NXT DEX

Continue development of the exchange and bring it toward release.

Later: NXT Finance

Define and build the DeFi suite on top of the launchpad and exchange.
